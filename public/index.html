THE WORKSHOP

Thanks for stopping by. Unicorn Taxidermy is a home studio project I started back in 2020.

After a long break from the NYC music scene—where I tried my hand as a session guitarist and producer—I started messing around with music again during the lockdowns.

It's strictly a hobby these days. No goals, no stress—just late-night synths, loud guitars, and whatever else feels right. It's basically a place for me to get some ideas out of my head.

Soo welcome to the workshop or something like that. And feel free to check out my old band Blip Blip Bleep and collaborations with Miles Fisher and Kayce Laine.
